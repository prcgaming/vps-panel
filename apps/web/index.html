Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our HVM cloud platform.

What is the difference between HVM and Containers?

HVM (Hardware Virtual Machine) runs a completely isolated operating system kernel via KVM/QEMU with dedicated virtual hardware. Containers share the host kernel. HVM allows running Windows, custom kernels, Docker, and VPNs.

How do I log into my virtual machine?

You can access your VPS instantly via standard SSH using your IPv4 address and root credentials, or via the interactive Web Serial Console directly inside the Client Panel.

Can I upgrade or resize my VPS later?

Yes. You can scale CPU cores, RAM, and NVMe disk space anytime from the control panel with zero data loss.

Where do I find support or report issues?

You can connect with our community and support team through our official Discord and community channels.
